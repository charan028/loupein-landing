What the chart already holds

One agent walks the whole record

The Skeptic answers in types

Every candidate faces TypeSafe's Jev model. Not a paragraph of hedging, a typed judgment: SURFACE, DISMISS, or VERIFY. If nothing survives, the dentist hears nothing. Silence is a valid result.
